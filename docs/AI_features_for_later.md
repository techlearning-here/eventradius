User-side AI
Personalized event feed: recommend events based on stated interests, past clicks, bookings, time of day, budget, and distance preferences.

Smart notifications: predict when a user is most likely to engage and send alerts only when the match is strong, such as “40% off movie nearby tonight.”

Natural-language search: let users type “cheap movie tonight near me” or “family events this weekend under $20,” and convert that into structured filters and ranked results.

Explainable recommendations: show why an event is suggested, such as genre match, nearby location, friend interest, or price drop, which helps trust and conversions.

Itinerary builder: assemble a mini plan like movie + dinner + nearby dessert based on timing, distance, and current offers.

Venue-side AI
Dynamic pricing suggestions: recommend discount levels for low-demand slots based on occupancy, timing, local demand, and historical conversion patterns.

Demand forecasting: predict likely attendance for each showtime or slot so owners can decide when to discount and by how much.

Promotion targeting: identify which user segments are price-sensitive, family-oriented, or likely to attend on short notice, then match offers to them.

Campaign optimization: automatically test headlines, images, price points, and send times to improve booking rates.

Marketplace AI
Supply-demand matching: rank events not just by popularity, but by “who is likely to go right now,” which is especially useful for empty cinema seats and off-peak inventory.

Cross-category recommendations: if someone likes movies, the system can also surface comedy shows, food offers, or family activities with similar timing and price sensitivity.

Lead scoring for venues: tell owners which listings are underperforming and which users are most likely to convert if shown a stronger offer.

Fraud and quality detection: identify fake events, duplicate listings, suspicious pricing changes, or poor-quality organizer submissions before they go live.
