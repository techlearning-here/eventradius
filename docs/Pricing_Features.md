# EventRadius Pricing Features

## Overview

EventRadius operates on a **freemium model** with clear separation between **event discoverers** (always free) and **event organizers** (tiered pricing).

> **Key Principle:** We don't sell tickets. We connect event seekers with organizers and help organizers maximize attendance through AI-powered dynamic pricing and real-time notifications.

---

## Event Discoverer (100% FREE)

All features completely free for event seekers. No credit card required. Ever.

### Free Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Discover Events** | Browse unlimited events by location, category, date, and interests |
| **Hyper-Local Search** | Find events near you with precise location filtering and radius settings |
| **Event Registration** | Register for any event instantly — free entry, paid events, or RSVP (zero platform fees) |
| **Save Favorites** | Bookmark events, get reminders before they start |
| **Smart Notifications** | Get notified about new events matching your interests |
| **Event Chat** | Connect with other attendees before, during, and after events |

**Revenue Model:** Discoverers never pay. Revenue comes from organizers via subscriptions and AI commission on dynamic-priced sales.

## AI Commission Explained

### What is it?
Commission is charged **only on dynamic priced events**. Regular price events have **zero commission**.

| Tier | AI Commission | Regular Price Events |
|------|---------------|---------------------|
| **Free** | N/A (no AI) | $0 |
| **Starter ($5)** | 2% | $0 |
| **Pro ($15)** | 1% | $0 |
| **Enterprise ($49)** | 0.5% | $0 |

### Example Scenario
**Cinema Tuesday 7pm Showing:**
- 100-seat theater, 2 hours before showtime, only 30 seats sold
- **AI Action:** Drops price 40% ($12 → $7.20) and instantly notifies 200 nearby movie fans via push notification
- **Result:** 40 additional seats filled
- **Revenue:** $288 additional income (vs $0 if seats stayed empty)
- **Commission (Pro):** $2.88 (1% of $288) — **Net gain: $285.12**

### Why It Works
- **Dynamic pricing without real-time alerts is worthless**
- AI identifies interested subscribers and pushes instant notifications
- **Speed is everything** — alerts reach users in seconds
- Commission is tiny compared to revenue generated from filled seats
