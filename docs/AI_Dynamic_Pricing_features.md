# Dynamic Event Features – Feature Breakdown

## 1. Dynamic Pricing Engine

- *Rule-based dynamic pricing*  
  - Automatically adjust prices based on time, demand, inventory, and event type.  
- *Price bands / guardrails*  
  - Let organizers set minimum and maximum price limits so discounts or surges stay within acceptable bounds.  
- *Time-based pricing*  
  - Apply weekday, weekend, morning, evening, holiday, or last-minute pricing logic.  
- *Inventory-based pricing*  
  - Lower or raise prices depending on unsold seats / slots / tickets remaining.  
- *Demand-based pricing*  
  - Adjust pricing based on booking velocity, views, search activity, and expected turnout.  
- *Early-bird / late-stage pricing*  
  - Reward early bookings or push final-hour deals to fill unsold capacity.  

## 2. Perishable Inventory Management

- *Empty-seat / empty-slot detection*  
  - Identify underbooked showtimes, classes, tables, or private sessions in real time.  
- *Time-to-expiry tracking*  
  - Measure how close an event or slot is to becoming worthless because the time window is about to pass.  
- *Capacity health indicator*  
  - Show organizers whether an event is underfilled, healthy, or near sold out.  
- *Automatic discount triggers*  
  - If a threshold is met (for example, 40% unsold 4 hours before start), automatically activate a deal.  
- *Last-minute inventory release*  
  - Push unsold capacity into “Tonight,” “Near you now,” or “Last-minute deals” sections.

## 3. Smart Offer Types

- *Last-minute deals*  
  - Discount unsold capacity close to event time.  
- *Off-peak offers*  
  - Encourage demand for weekday, matinee, morning, or low-demand time windows.  
- *Family bundles*  
  - Offer price bundles for adults + kids or group attendance.  
- *Group-fill offers*  
  - Discounts activate when a certain number of attendees book together.  
- *Flash promotions*  
  - Short-lived offers with urgency timers.  
- *Package pricing*  
  - Bundle event + food + extras or multi-event access into one offer.

## 4. Organizer Controls

- *Pricing rules builder*  
  - Configure when and why pricing changes happen, using conditions and thresholds.  
- *Manual override*  
  - Organizers can pause or override AI/recommended pricing anytime.  
- *Approval-based automation*  
  - The system suggests discounts, but organizers approve them before publishing.  
- *Schedule-based rule presets*  
  - Prebuilt templates for cinemas, workshops, restaurants, wellness slots, etc.  
- *Offer visibility controls*  
  - Choose whether a deal is public, limited to selected users, or only sent through notifications.  
- *Capacity reservation rules*  
  - Hold back some inventory from discounting if needed.

## 5. AI Demand Forecasting

- *Attendance prediction*  
  - Forecast expected turnout for each event or time slot.  
- *Demand score*  
  - Give each listing a score such as low / medium / high expected demand.  
- *Booking velocity monitoring*  
  - Track how quickly tickets or slots are being taken and compare against expected pace.  
- *Best discount recommendation*  
  - Suggest whether to offer 10%, 20%, or 40% based on expected conversion uplift.  
- *Best send-time recommendation*  
  - Predict when to push a notification or promotion for maximum uptake.  
- *Seasonality / local signal analysis*  
  - Factor in day of week, local events, weather, holidays, and trends.

## 6. User Discovery & Matching

- *Radius-based event matching*  
  - Show users events and deals within a chosen distance from them.  
- *Time-window matching*  
  - Recommend events happening “now,” “tonight,” “this weekend,” or within user availability.  
- *Budget-aware recommendations*  
  - Match events to a user’s spending preference and highlight discounted options.  
- *Interest-based recommendations*  
  - Use past clicks, bookings, categories, and saved preferences to personalize discovery.  
- *Deal sensitivity matching*  
  - Identify users more likely to respond to discounts and surface dynamic offers to them.  
- *Cross-category suggestions*  
  - Recommend nearby alternatives like movie + dinner, workshop + café, etc.

## 7. Dynamic Notifications & Urgency

- *Smart push alerts*  
  - Notify the right users when a nearby event drops in price or has open capacity.  
- *Urgency messaging*  
  - Messages like “Starts in 2 hours,” “Only 8 seats left,” or “40% off until 6 PM.”  
- *Watchlist / alert subscriptions*  
  - Users can follow a venue, category, or price threshold.  
- *Drop alerts*  
  - Notify when price falls below a user’s preferred level.  
- *Quiet-hour and frequency controls*  
  - Avoid spam and let users control how often they are notified.

## 8. Offer Presentation & UX

- *Dynamic pricing badge*  
  - Mark listings with labels like “Last-minute deal,” “Off-peak special,” or “Price dropped.”  
- *Original vs current price display*  
  - Clearly show discount amount and savings.  
- *Reason-for-offer explanation*  
  - Explain why a discount exists, such as low-demand showtime or special weekday offer.  
- *Countdown timers*  
  - Show how long an offer remains active.  
- *Trust messaging*  
  - Make pricing feel fair and rule-based so users do not feel manipulated.  

## 9. Organizer Analytics Dashboard

- *Revenue vs occupancy view*  
  - Compare earnings and fill-rate for each event or slot.  
- *Discount performance analysis*  
  - See how each offer affected bookings, revenue, and utilization.  
- *Conversion by audience segment*  
  - Track which user groups respond best to dynamic offers.  
- *Pricing experiment results*  
  - Compare different discount strategies and outcomes.  
- *Channel performance*  
  - Measure results from app feed, push alerts, SMS, email, etc.  
- *Forecast vs actual*  
  - Compare predicted attendance to real turnout for learning and model tuning.

## 10. Dynamic Event Types to Support

- *Cinemas*  
  - Empty weekday seats, matinees, late-night showtimes.  
- *Workshops & classes*  
  - Spare spots in cooking, language, coding, art, or fitness sessions.  
- *Restaurants & food events*  
  - Off-peak seating, tasting nights, chef-table events.  
- *Wellness & beauty appointments*  
  - Last-minute salon, spa, massage, or consultation slots.  
- *Tours & activities*  
  - Walking tours, guided visits, local excursions, escape rooms.  
- *Micro-events*  
  - Small community events, talks, pop-ups, and local gatherings.  

## 11. Safeguards & Trust Controls

- *Min-price floor*  
  - Prevent pricing from dropping too low.  
- *Discount frequency limits*  
  - Avoid training customers to wait for discounts.  
- *Eligibility rules*  
  - Some offers can be limited to first-time users, nearby users, or selected segments.  
- *Transparent pricing policy*  
  - Explain how pricing works to preserve trust.  
- *Host review queue*  
  - Give organizers a final review step before major price changes go live.

## 12. MVP Recommendation

For a first version, prioritize:

1. Dynamic pricing rules with min/max guardrails.  
2. Empty-seat / empty-slot detection.  
3. Last-minute and off-peak offers.  
4. Radius-based user discovery for discounted events.  
5. Smart notifications for nearby deals.  
6. Organizer dashboard with occupancy, bookings, and discount results.  
7. Forecasting-lite recommendations (basic demand score + suggested discount).
