# Private Event Mode

## What is it

Private Event Mode is an invite-only event workflow inside EventRadius for personal and family events such as weddings, birthdays, memorials, baby showers, anniversaries, reunions, and religious gatherings.  
Unlike public events, these events are not shown in public listings, search, or discovery feeds and can only be accessed by invited guests through a private link and simple validation using email or phone number.

## What problem are we solving

Planning private events is often fragmented and stressful because hosts manage guests across WhatsApp chats, spreadsheets, calls, and scattered RSVP messages.  
This creates repeated operational problems such as unclear guest counts, missing RSVPs, confusion around plus-ones and households, difficulty tracking meal preferences, and too much manual follow-up.  
Hosts also need privacy and control, especially for family or sensitive gatherings, but many existing workflows are not designed specifically for hidden, invite-only event coordination.

## How we solve it

EventRadius Private Event Mode gives hosts a single private event page that they can share only with invited guests through WhatsApp, SMS, email, or direct link.  
Guests open the link, validate their invited phone number or email, and then submit structured RSVP information instead of sending loose messages back to the host.

## What guests can do

- Confirm whether they are attending or not.
- Enter the number of adults and kids attending from the same household.
- Add names of attending family members if needed.
- Select food preferences and meal choices.
- Enter allergies or dietary restrictions.
- Mention accommodation, transport, or other special needs.
- Choose which functions or ceremonies they will attend if the event has multiple parts.

## What organizers get

- A private, non-public event page with invite-only access.
- A structured guest list instead of scattered replies.
- Live RSVP tracking with confirmed, declined, and pending guests.
- Household-wise attendance breakdown for better family event planning.
- Meal and dietary summaries for caterers and planners.
- Fewer follow-up calls, fewer missed details, and better planning confidence.

## Why this matters

Private events are not just about sending invites; they require accurate planning around people, families, food, logistics, and privacy.  
By replacing WhatsApp chaos and spreadsheet tracking with a single invite-only workflow, EventRadius helps hosts plan personal events with more clarity, less stress, and better data.

## In one sentence

Private Event Mode helps hosts run invite-only family and personal events with private access, structured RSVPs, household attendance tracking, and planning-friendly guest data instead of messy manual coordination.
